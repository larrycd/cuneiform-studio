(words, parts of speech, English glosses, inflected forms, cuneiform spellings and Sumerograms), as
extracted by Wiktextract and published at https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Akkadian/ and
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Sumerian/ (extraction of the enwiktionary dump dated 2026-10-01).

inflected forms were kept, and Sumerian spellings were limited to signs the font can draw.
