About

Cuneiform Studio writes Akkadian and Sumerian in cuneiform and reads it back. It was designed and built by Larry Dalton.

  • Akkadian (Babylonian and Assyrian) is written syllable by syllable, as the scribes did: šarrum "king" becomes šar-ru-um.
  • Sumerian words are written with their dictionary signs, mostly whole-word signs such as LUGAL "king".
  • English → translate gives a rule-based draft in Akkadian (subject, object, verb last; case endings; past tense with person prefixes; the stative for "is") or Sumerian (subject with -e, object, verb with mu-; copula -am₃), with a confidence score.
  • English by sound writes any English word or name in syllable signs, the way foreign names were written in Babylonia. It is one-way: it reads back as syllables, with likely source words.
  • Ancient columns turn the text 90° clockwise, giving columns read top to bottom and right to left, as on early monuments.
