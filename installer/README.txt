Cuneiform Studio 1.0.0 - by Larry Dalton

Write and read Akkadian and Sumerian cuneiform, on your own computer.
- Akkadian words spelled by syllable, Sumerian words with their dictionary signs
- English words and names written by sound
- English to Akkadian or Sumerian translation drafts (rule-based, with a confidence score)
- Lines or ancient columns; reads SVG, PNG and JPEG back

Install: extract this folder, then double-click Install.cmd.
The studio installs for your Windows account only (no administrator rights), runs at http://localhost:8768/
and starts automatically when you sign in. The infographic is copied to your Desktop.

Uninstall: run Uninstall.ps1 from %LOCALAPPDATA%\CuneiformStudio, or use Settings > Apps if you used the setup program.
